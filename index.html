About the artist

Marc

Marc is a contemporary artist exploring pop culture, luxury and identity through bold, narrative-driven compositions.

Working in graffiti and acrylic, the artist reinterprets familiar imagery in vivid colour, drawing on fashion, street art and modern visual culture. Every work is an original painting.
